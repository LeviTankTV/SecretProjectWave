Zone Wall Editor

Walls block both players and mobs — so you set each zone's "mob zone" by hand. Zone local coordinates: x from 0 to 8000, y from 0 to 5000 (y grows downward). At the bottom of a zone is the Safe Zone (light band), above it a divider wall with a gap in the center. Red rectangles are your walls. Game →
